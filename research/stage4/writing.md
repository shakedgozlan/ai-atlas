## Training on user data

### Takeaway
Gamma, Grammarly/Superhuman and free DeepL use user content for training by default; Gamma and Superhuman offer opt-outs. Paid DeepL does not train on content. Jasper only says it does not let third-party LLMs train on customer data. For QuillBot, Wordtune, Beautiful.ai and Napkin, nothing was read.

### Cited Findings
- **Gamma:** For individual accounts (Free, Plus, Pro, Ultra) the default is "Data is allowed to be used to train AI models"; users can opt out under Account settings > Data Controls. For Business and Enterprise accounts, data is excluded and the setting is locked. — [Gamma help: training](https://help.gamma.app/en/articles/12281928-does-gamma-use-my-content-to-train-its-ai-features)
- **Superhuman (Grammarly):** "We also use information we collect to train our AI models", with user control via "training control(s) in your account settings". AI service providers are restricted from training on user content. The policy is effective November 16, 2026, and the entity is Superhuman Platform Inc. — [Superhuman privacy policy](https://superhuman.com/legal/privacy-policy); [Grammarly responsible AI](https://grammarly.com/ai/responsible-ai)
- **DeepL:** Free Translator and Write content is used to "train and improve our neural networks and algorithms". With Pro, API Pro and Write Pro, "your texts will not be used to improve the quality of our services". — [DeepL privacy](https://www.deepl.com/privacy); [DeepL Pro](https://www.deepl.com/en/pro)
- **Jasper:** "Your company data and outputs are never used to train third-party LLMs." The page says nothing about Jasper's own models. — [Jasper trust](https://jasper.ai/trust)

### Gaps
- QuillBot, Wordtune, Beautiful.ai and Napkin privacy policies were not read.
- Commercial-use terms were not read for any tool.

## Hebrew support

### Takeaway
Hebrew is officially documented only for Gamma (RTL generation and editing), DeepL (translation) and QuillBot (Translator only). Grammarly's language list omits Hebrew. For Wordtune, Jasper and Napkin, no source mentions Hebrew. For Beautiful.ai, Hebrew was not checked.

### Cited Findings
- **Gamma:** "Gamma supports right-to-left (RTL) languages, including Arabic, Farsi, Hebrew, and Urdu!" Mixed RTL and LTR text "may require manual adjustments". — [Gamma help: RTL](https://help.gamma.app/en/articles/11016519-does-gamma-support-right-to-left-languages)
- **DeepL:** Hebrew is supported by the next-generation model. Glossary, tone and alternatives are excluded for the added languages "except for Hebrew and Vietnamese". — [DeepL languages](https://support.deepl.com/hc/en-us/articles/360019925219-Languages-supported-by-DeepL)
- **QuillBot:** Hebrew is listed only for the Translator (34 languages). The Paraphraser supports 23 languages, Grammar Checker works in EN/DE/ES/FR, and the Summarizer only in English. — [QuillBot languages](https://help.quillbot.com/hc/en-us/articles/4541525640727-What-languages-does-QuillBot-work-in)
- **Grammarly:** Suggestions are available in 24 languages besides English, and Hebrew is not among them. — [Superhuman help: languages](https://help.superhuman.com/hc/en-us/articles/46242287887757-Does-Grammarly-support-languages-other-than-English)
- **Napkin:** The site's language menu lists EN, ES, FR, IT, KO and PT only. — [Napkin pricing](https://www.napkin.ai/pricing)

### Inferences
- QuillBot's `filters.hebrew.label` was set to "עברית במתרגם בלבד" instead of the standard "עברית נתמכת", to avoid implying that its core writing tools work in Hebrew.

### Gaps
- Wordtune, Jasper and Beautiful.ai language pages were not read.
