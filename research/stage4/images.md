## Which tools belong in the category, and why

### Takeaway
Ten tools made the list: Midjourney, Adobe Firefly, FLUX (Black Forest Labs), Ideogram, Leonardo.Ai, Magnific (formerly Freepik AI), Krea, Stability AI, Topaz Labs and Photoroom. Reve is out because several reports say its image generation shut down in September 2026, and the official sources I could read did not settle this. Krea and Magnific are both IN.

### Why Magnific and Krea are (now) in the atlas (answer for the site owner)
- **Magnific**: it is in. Magnific is the new name of Freepik's AI platform. Per magnific.com, on April 28, 2026 Freepik "brought all its AI tools together under one name: Magnific", and "Your Freepik account, plan, and credits work here". Pricing was taken only from the official magnific.com/pricing: Premium $20/mo or $14.50/mo yearly, Plus $45 or $33.75, Pro $110 or $82.50. Credits are counted per year, and prices exclude VAT. The verification is partial for two reasons: the Pro credit tiers are garbled on the official page, and no free plan is shown. It appears in 5/6 roundups and as Freepik on the a16z list. It may have been missing earlier because it was listed under the old name Freepik, and because third-party prices conflicted. Both issues are now resolved with the official page. — [Magnific rename page](https://www.magnific.com/freepik-ai); [Magnific pricing](https://www.magnific.com/pricing)

Condition A is met: Krea 2, "our first foundation model built completely from scratch", was published May 12, 2026, and the pricing page also shows "Krea 3" labelled NEW, with no date. Condition B is met: two independent roundups in the window cover Krea, Zapier (updated October 2026) and Rework (updated Jul 16, 2026). The earlier ambiguity remains for the regular price, and it should be re-checked after the promotion ends. — [Krea pricing](https://www.krea.ai/pricing); [Krea 2 blog](https://canvas.krea.ai/blog/krea-2-image-model); [Rework](https://resources.rework.com/tools/ai-tools/best-ai-image-generators-2026); [Zapier](https://zapier.com/blog/best-ai-image-generator/)

## Pricing, free tier and verification per tool

### Takeaway
Two records are "אומת ממקור רשמי" (Midjourney, FLUX) and eight are "אומת חלקית" (Adobe Firefly, Ideogram, Leonardo.Ai, Magnific, Krea, Stability AI, Topaz Labs, Photoroom). None is "לא אומת". Promotions were running at Adobe (Pro / Pro Plus), Krea (Black Friday) and Topaz (annual plans).

### Cited Findings
- Midjourney: Basic $10/mo or $8/mo yearly, Standard $30/$24 (15 h fast), Pro $60/$48 (30 h, Stealth), Mega $120/$96 (60 h). Yearly is "20% off". There is no free plan on the page. — [MJ pricing](https://www.midjourney.com/pricing)
- Adobe Firefly: Free has "Limited free daily generations". Standard US$9.99/mo with 2,000 credits. Pro is regularly US$19.99 and Pro Plus regularly US$49.99, with promos of 50% and 60% for the first 6 months, ending Dec 6. Premium US$199.99/mo (team, annual). Students get no separate Firefly price; there is a link to "Students & Teachers Save 77% on Creative Cloud". — [Firefly plans](https://www.adobe.com/products/firefly/plans.html)
- FLUX: "No subscriptions, no seat fees". FLUX.2 [pro] costs $0.03 for the first MP. FLUX 3 Image costs $0.041 to $0.607 per image, and FLUX 3 Image Klein from $0.008. — [BFL pricing](https://bfl.ai/pricing)
- Ideogram: Free gets weekly slow credits "for eligible accounts". Plus is $15/mo with 1,000 priority credits. Pro shows $42/mo (struck from $60), or $504 billed annually, with 3,500 credits. "We don't offer discounts or free trials for subscription plans." — [Ideogram pricing](https://ideogram.ai/pricing/)
- Leonardo.Ai: Free gets 150 Fast Tokens daily, and "All images are public". Apprentice $12 / $10, Artisan Unlimited $30 / $24, Maestro Unlimited $60 / $48. The page does not mark which price is the yearly one. The API starts with a $5 credit. — [Leonardo pricing](https://www.leonardo.ai/pricing)
- Magnific: see the paragraph above. — [Magnific pricing](https://www.magnific.com/pricing)
- Krea: see the paragraph above. — [Krea pricing](https://www.krea.ai/pricing)
- Stability AI: "1 credit = $0.01" and "Get started with 25 free credits". Ultra costs 8 credits, SD 3.5 Large 6.5, SD 3.5 Flash 2.5. — [Stability pricing](https://docs.stability.ai/pricing)
- Topaz Labs: Topaz for Web – Image $19/mo. Gigapixel $29/mo, or $149/yr (promo $127). Photo $39/mo. Studio $79/mo (also shown as $69). Many annual prices are promotional, some "First year only". Commercial use is limited to organisations "under $1M USD annual revenue" except on Pro plans. — [Topaz pricing](https://www.topazlabs.com/pricing)
- Photoroom: Pro $7.50, Max $20.99, Ultra $82.50 per month, billed yearly. Monthly prices are not shown. Pro and Max show "Start free trial". The free plan is not described. — [Photoroom pricing](https://www.photoroom.com/pricing)

## Ownership, commercial rights and training on user data

### Takeaway
Only Adobe has an official statement verified on training ("Firefly models never train on what you create or upload"). For every other tool, the training policy is unverified: terms and privacy pages were not reached because of the fetch budget, or were blocked (Midjourney docs).

### Cited Findings
- Adobe: "Adobe does not train Adobe Firefly models or partner models on any Creative Cloud subscribers' personal content". Firefly outputs are "safe for commercial use". For partner models, "You're responsible for determining whether content generated by partner models is appropriate". — [Firefly page](https://www.adobe.com/products/firefly.html); [Firefly plans](https://www.adobe.com/products/firefly/plans.html)
- Ideogram: "We do not claim ownership of your generated images, and you're free to use them for any purpose, including commercial use." — [Ideogram pricing](https://ideogram.ai/pricing/)
- Leonardo.Ai, free plan: Leonardo holds rights to use, reproduce, modify and distribute the images, and the user gets a "non-exclusive, royalty-free licence" for commercial use. — [Leonardo pricing](https://www.leonardo.ai/pricing)
- Midjourney: "General commercial terms" on all plans. Stealth mode only on Pro and Mega. — [MJ pricing](https://www.midjourney.com/pricing)
- Magnific: "Commercial AI license" on all paid plans, plus music rights on Plus and Pro. — [Magnific pricing](https://www.magnific.com/pricing)
- Krea: commercial license on the paid cards, but not on the Free card. — [Krea pricing](https://www.krea.ai/pricing)
- Topaz: the revenue threshold of $1M USD, above which a Pro plan is needed. — [Topaz pricing](https://www.topazlabs.com/pricing)

### Inferences
- None beyond the above.

### Gaps
- Training policy and full terms for Midjourney, FLUX, Ideogram, Leonardo, Magnific, Krea, Stability, Topaz and Photoroom.
