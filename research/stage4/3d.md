## Which tools belong in the category, and are they alive?

### Takeaway
I kept all six starting candidates. All are live. Meshy, Tripo and Hyper3D Rodin pass the new-and-notable rule. Spline is in as a leading, established tool. Marble and Hunyuan 3D each appear in only one of the four counted roundups, so both fail rule B. The swap note under Inferences covers what to do about them.

## Pricing, free plans and credits

### Takeaway
Verification levels: Rodin is fully verified from the official page. Meshy, Spline, Marble and Hunyuan are partly verified. Tripo is not verified, because every page on tripo3d.ai returned client_error.

### Cited Findings
- **Meshy**: Free $0 with 100 credits/month, reset on the 1st at 00:00 UTC, up to 10 assets and 10 Meshy 6 Lite downloads a month. Paid plans: Pro $20 (1,000 credits), Premium $40 (3,000), Ultra $100 (8,000), Studio $70 (5,500 shared). The yearly toggle shows the same figures, while the FAQ says "20% off on annual plans", so yearly pricing is not verified. Promotion: 50% off the first month. Export formats: fbx, obj, usdz, glb, stl, blend, 3mf. — [Meshy pricing](https://meshy.ai/pricing); [Meshy docs](https://docs.meshy.ai/en/webapp/pricing). A Starter plan (250 credits, 20 downloads) exists for "select developing countries", with no price shown. — [Meshy help](https://help.meshy.ai/en/articles/12062933-what-are-your-prices-and-plans-offered-and-do-you-have-monthly-annual-plans)
- **Hyper3D Rodin**: Free; Creator $30/month monthly or $24/month yearly ("$288 today"); Business $120 or $96 ("$1,152"); Enterprise custom; Education (verify). Creator is "About 60 models", Business "About 416 models". Direct credits cost $1.5 each. The free plan allows export only from legacy models, keeps 10 private assets and has no API access. — [Hyper3D pricing](https://hyper3d.ai/pricing?lang=en)
- **Marble**: Free gives up to 4 worlds. Standard is $20 with 20,000 credits and 12 worlds. Pro is $35 with 25 worlds, textured-mesh export and commercial rights. Max gives up to 75 worlds, price not stated. These prices come from upgrade examples in the billing doc. Subscription credits do not roll over; top-up credits last 1 year and can be bought only on paid plans. — [Marble billing docs](https://docs.worldlabs.ai/marble/support/account-billing)
- **Marble API**: $1.00 per 1,250 credits, minimum $5. A marble-1.1 world costs 1,500 credits; an HQ mesh export costs 3,500. — [World Labs API pricing](https://docs.worldlabs.ai/api/pricing)
- **Spline**: per seat per month. Hobby $12 yearly / $15 monthly (the monthly price is shown struck through), Pro $25/$30, Max $60/$70. AI credits: 2,000 / 3,000 / 10,000 per seat. Free has no AI credits and puts a watermark on web exports. — [Spline pricing](https://www.spline.design/pricing)
- **Tripo API**: 1 credit = $0.01, minimum $1. Image-to-3D costs about 20 credits (about 30 with texture). — [Tripo API pricing](https://developers.tripo3d.ai/en/pricing)
- **Hunyuan via Tencent Cloud**: credit packs from $15 to $1,350 ($0.0135–0.015 per credit); a Normal generation costs 25 credits; the 1,000 free credits for new users ran until June 30, 2026. — [Tencent Cloud techpedia](https://www.tencentcloud.com/techpedia/148275?lang=en); [Tencent Cloud guide](https://www.tencentcloud.com/techpedia/148295?lang=en)
- Consumer prices for Hunyuan come from third parties only and were not used as prices: Basic free with 200 credits/month and Plus at $19.9/month (CGWORLD), and 20 free generations a day (Auganix). — [CGWORLD](https://cgworld.jp/flashnews/01-202602-TencentHY3D.html); [Auganix](https://www.auganix.org/?p=17899)

## Licences, commercial rights and training on user data

### Takeaway
Meshy (CC BY 4.0) and Marble (non-commercial only) explicitly restrict free-plan output, and Rodin keeps unlimited export and "any use" for paid plans. Meshy, Marble and Spline all allow use of user data for training: Meshy anonymized with an email opt-out, Marble with an opt-out for paid users only, and Spline de-identified with no opt-out found. Rodin's terms only mention "improve our services, and conduct and support research".

### Cited Findings
- **Meshy**: Free outputs are CC BY 4.0 (commercial use with attribution). Paid users get "full private ownership" unless they publish to the Community. — [Meshy help: ownership](https://help.meshy.ai/en/articles/10137554-what-is-the-ownership-of-the-generated-models). For non-Enterprise plans, "Meshy may use anonymized user data for future model training"; the opt-out is an email to support@meshy.ai; Enterprise data is not used. — [Meshy help: training](https://help.meshy.ai/en/articles/15724182-is-meshy-safe-and-private-data-and-training-faq)
- **Rodin**: Deemos "will not limit your use of such Output" and gives no copyrightability warranty. Prompts and outputs may be used to "provide, maintain and improve our services, and conduct and support research". The company is Deemos Corporation, Delaware; another section points to Hong Kong courts; the terms carry no revision date. — [Hyper3D terms](https://hyper3d.ai/legal/terms)
- **Marble**: World Labs ToS, "Last updated date: January 21, 2026". For free accounts, World Labs retains the rights and the user gets a non-commercial licence. Paid users own outputs and may use them commercially. The user-content licence includes training; it is irrevocable for free users, and paid users can opt out via the Account page. — [World Labs ToS](https://www.worldlabs.ai/terms-of-service). Conflict: the billing doc lists commercial rights only under Pro; the ToS wording, which covers all paid plans, was preferred. — [Marble billing docs](https://docs.worldlabs.ai/marble/support/account-billing)
- **Spline**: Terms "Last modified on September 29, 2026", effective October 29, 2026. Users retain ownership of their User Content. De-identified and aggregated data may be used "to develop, train, evaluate, and improve machine learning models". — [Spline terms](https://spline.design/terms)

### Inferences
- None.

- Hunyuan consumer terms and the open-source licence (Hugging Face LICENSE) were not loaded.
