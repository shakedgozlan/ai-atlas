## Which tools belong in the category, and why

### Takeaway
Seven tools made the list: Canva, Adobe Express, Recraft, Looka, Kittl, Microsoft Designer and Adobe Illustrator. Lovart was dropped because I found no independent review of it. Every review I found was written by Lovart itself, by a competitor, or was paid sponsor content. Adobe Illustrator, an alternate on the brief, took its place as a leading, established tool.

## Per-tool key facts (pricing, rights, training, versions, Hebrew, students)

### Takeaway
Pricing was read from official pages for Microsoft Designer (US), Adobe Express (Singapore), Recraft (docs page), Kittl (yearly billing only) and Illustrator (Chile). Looka is only partly verified, and Canva not at all. Training on user content is documented for Recraft (on by default for Free and Pro) and Adobe (does not train on Creative Cloud subscribers' content). It is not documented for the others in the pages I read.

### Cited Findings

**Canva**
- Canva AI 2.0: "Canva AI will launch as a research preview on April 16, rolling out to the first one million people". Dateline SYDNEY & LOS ANGELES. The release does not say which plans include it, and says nothing on pricing or user data. — [Business Wire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260416696203/en)
- Hebrew: "he-IL | Hebrew" is in Canva's supported locales. The help page says "Design on Canva in your local language to unlock local fonts and design templates" but lists no languages and does not mention RTL. — [canva.dev locales](https://www.canva.dev/docs/extensions/reference/supported-locales); [Canva help](https://www.canva.com/help/language-settings/)

**Adobe Express** (prices in SGD, Singapore page)
- Free: S$0, "Free daily generations", 5GB, 345,000+ templates. Premium: S$13.39/mo, 250 credits, 30-day trial. Firefly Pro: S$26.79/mo, 4,000 credits, 7-day trial. Teams: S$11.10 per licence per month, annual plan billed monthly, 2-seat minimum (promotion S$6.93 for the first year). Enterprise: contact sales. — [Express pricing](https://www.adobe.com/express/pricing)
- Education: free for qualified K-12 educators. For students the page says "You might already have free access to Adobe Express Premium through your school". Higher education: "Contact Sales". Creative Cloud Pro for students: regular price S$91.13, S$26.79/mo for the first year then S$53.61/mo, "Students and teachers save 70%". A Black Friday banner runs until "Nov 27". — [Express pricing](https://www.adobe.com/express/pricing)
- Training: "No, we don't train on any Creative Cloud subscribers' personal content". Commercial use: "For features without the beta label, you can use Firefly-generated outputs in your commercial projects." — [Firefly FAQ](https://helpx.adobe.com/firefly/faq.html)
- MAX 2026 (11/10/2026): AI Assistant (beta), Generate Webpage (beta), Extract Brand (beta), Brand Check (beta), export to PSD/IDML (IDML in beta), and use inside Claude, ChatGPT, Copilot and Gemini. Runs on web, desktop and mobile. — [blog](https://www.adobe.com/express/learn/blog/takeaways-adobe-max-2026)
- Hebrew: not mentioned on any official page I read. Adobe community threads report RTL bugs in Express; these were seen only as search titles, not opened, and not used.

**Recraft**
- Plans (docs; the page names the pricing table as authoritative): Basic 1,000 credits $12/mo or $10/mo yearly ($120/yr); Pro 2,000 credits $20 or $16, 4,000 $40 or $32, 8,000 $80 or $64, 16,000 $160 or $128; Teams from $22 or $18. The Teams 8,000-credit tier shows $70 monthly but $88 yearly (internal inconsistency). "All paid plans include private image generation and commercial usage rights while subscribed." — [paid plans](https://www.recraft.ai/docs/plans-and-billing/paid-plans)
- Free: "Images generated on the Free plan are owned by Recraft", publicly visible, "not licensed for commercial use". — [pricing](https://www.recraft.ai/pricing). Credits "reset monthly (or daily for Free users)". Vector generation is included on every plan. V4.1 costs 2 credits per image, V4.1 Vector 4. — [credits](https://www.recraft.ai/docs/plans-and-billing/credits)
- Training: user images, uploads and prompts are used by default. "Training opt-out is not available on the Free plan". Pro can opt out. Team has the opt-out applied by default. "API inputs and outputs are never used for model training." — [data use](https://www.recraft.ai/docs/trust-and-security/data-use-and-model-training)

**Looka**
- Brand Kit costs "$96 USD/year (billed annually)", and Brand Kit Web costs "$129 USD/year". It renews annually. "you'll always own the rights to your logo, even after your subscription ends." — [Brand Kit](https://help.looka.com/en/articles/4636515-what-is-the-brand-kit)
- The Basic package is one PNG of 1000x1000px. Premium adds PNG, PDF, EPS and SVG files and black, white and transparent versions. Prices are not stated. — [packages](https://help.looka.com/en/articles/485739-what-s-included-in-each-of-the-packages)
- "full ownership", but copyright "applies to your logo as a whole and not the individual elements". For a trademark, consult an attorney. — [ownership](https://help.looka.com/en/articles/484949-do-i-own-the-full-copyright-to-my-purchased-logo)

**Kittl**
- Pro costs $12.00, Expert $26.00 and Max $48.00, "per editor billed annually"; monthly billing prices were not shown. Expert gets 200 daily tokens and Max 600. Free is personal use only. Pro and Expert carry a commercial licence "up to 500,000 copies". — [pricing](https://www.kittl.com/pricing). The official llms.txt gives "approximate" prices that conflict (Pro about $19 monthly, about $14 yearly). — [llms.txt](https://www.kittl.com/llms.txt)
- AI rights: Pro and Expert users own their AI images. Free-plan AI images enter the Kittl Content library, and Free users may sell designs with "provided by Kittl". This conflicts with the pricing page. — [help](https://help.kittl.com/licensing/ai-design-rights/)
- June 12, 2026 update: Brand, Remix Styles, Ideogram 4, with "multilingual text" in Ideogram 4. — [blog](https://www.kittl.com/blogs/kittl-product-update-12th-jun-2026/)

**Microsoft Designer**
- Free tier with monthly credits ("a fresh set of credits at the start of each month"; 1 credit per Copilot interaction). Microsoft 365 Personal costs $9.99/mo or $99.99/yr, Family $12.99/mo or $129.99/yr, and Premium $19.99/mo or $199.99/yr. These plans give "up to 4X more credits". Designer features are integrated into Photos, Word and PowerPoint. — [product page](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/microsoft-designer)
- "Designer is licensed for personal, non-commercial use with your Microsoft account." Data is used "to provide, to support, and to improve the Designer service", and training is not explicitly addressed. It runs on web, iOS and Android. "any text in the generated images may not be in the requested language." The page was updated 2026-09-11 per its metadata. — [FAQ](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/designer/frequently-asked-questions-about-microsoft-designer)
- Third-party news (not opened, search titles only): the Designer bot in Teams was retired around February 27, 2026 (Message Center MC1197104). This does not affect the standalone app.

**Adobe Illustrator** (prices in CLP, Chile page, excluding VAT, annual plans billed monthly)
- Illustrator costs Ch$ 15.640/mo with 25 credits and a 7-day trial. Creative Cloud Pro costs Ch$ 46.250/mo regular (Black Friday price Ch$ 19.818) with 4,000 credits. Creative Cloud Pro for students and teachers costs Ch$ 10.875/mo (76% off the first year, until Nov 27). For teams, Illustrator costs Ch$ 32.810 and Creative Cloud Pro Ch$ 91.150 per licence. — [plans](https://www.adobe.com/products/illustrator/plans.html)
- MAX 2026, dated "Tuesday, November 10, 2026": Creative Agent (beta) in Illustrator, on-canvas Blend controls, and local MCP planned for a private beta in early 2027. — [Adobe PR](https://news.adobe.com/news/2026/11/adobe-transforms-creative-cloud-tools)
