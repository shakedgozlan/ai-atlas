## Final tool list (9)

### Takeaway
Nine tools: five meeting notetakers (Otter, Fireflies, Granola, Fathom, Read AI), one dictation/notetaker hybrid (Wispr Flow), two knowledge tools (Notion, Gemini Notebook, formerly NotebookLM) and one data-analysis tool (Julius). tl;dv was dropped in favour of Wispr Flow to cover dictation. Fathom was acquired by Superhuman ("September 14, 2026"); the product continues ("Nothing changes today for Fathom users") — [Fathom post](https://www.fathom.ai/superhuman).

### Cited Findings
- **Otter** (`otter`): leading meeting transcriber with a renewing free plan (300 min/month) and a 20% .edu discount; Hebrew listed only as "coming soon" in the Nov 10, 2026 language expansion. — [pricing](https://otter.ai/pricing); [27 languages blog](https://otter.ai/blog/otter-ai-announces-its-biggest-multilingual-leap-yet-27-new-languages-and-multilingual-capabilities)

Fireflies Talk (free dictation) Sep 29, 2026. — [pricing](https://fireflies.ai/pricing); [languages](https://guide.fireflies.ai/articles/2973706448-learn-about-fireflies-supported-languages); [privacy guide](https://guide.fireflies.ai/articles/7434774675-responsible-and-secure-meeting-notetaking-with-fireflies); [Talk](https://fireflies.ai/blog/introducing-fireflies-talk)
- **Granola** (`granola`): bot-free notepad that uses the computer's audio; Hebrew on desktop only; student offer limited to US, UK, Canada. — [pricing](https://www.granola.ai/pricing); [languages](https://docs.granola.ai/help-center/customising-granola/multi-language.md); [students](https://www.granola.ai/students); [updates](https://www.granola.ai/updates)
- **Fathom** (`fathom`): unlimited free recording and transcription, bot or bot-free (beta, Mac); Hebrew not in its 38-language list; part of Superhuman since Sep 14, 2026; platform update with bot-free capture, public API and MCP (Business Wire release, URL dated 20260415, no date in text). — [pricing](https://www.fathom.ai/pricing); [languages](https://help.fathom.video/en/articles/296192); [security](https://help.fathom.video/en/articles/296512); [Superhuman post](https://www.fathom.ai/superhuman); [Business Wire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260415965820/en/Fathom-Unveils-Major-Platform-Update-Adding-Bot-Free-Capture-Account-Wide-Meeting-Insights-and-Widely-Expanded-LLM-Integrations)

## Hebrew support

### Takeaway
Officially supported: Fireflies (transcription and summaries), Granola (desktop only), Wispr Flow (dictation, manual selection), Gemini Notebook (output language), Read AI (listed in a March 2026 post). Not yet available: Otter (Hebrew "coming soon"). Not mentioned: Fathom (absent from a 38-language list), Julius. Not verified: Notion (help page contradicts itself).

### Cited Findings
- Fireflies: "48 | Hebrew | he"; the guide covers transcription and summaries. — [Fireflies languages](https://guide.fireflies.ai/articles/2973706448-learn-about-fireflies-supported-languages)
- Granola: "Granola supports 31 languages on desktop (macOS and Windows) and 17 languages on mobile (iOS and Android)"; Hebrew is in the desktop list only. The updates page says 32 languages (Sep 2, 2026), a small conflict. — [Granola docs](https://docs.granola.ai/help-center/customising-granola/multi-language.md); [updates](https://www.granola.ai/updates)
- Wispr Flow: "Other selectable languages—including Urdu, Hebrew, Ukrainian". — [Wispr docs](https://docs.wisprflow.ai/articles/3191899797-use-flow-with-multiple-languages)
- Gemini Notebook: Hebrew in the output-language list; "Different features may support different languages." — [help](https://support.google.com/gemininotebook/answer/16261963?hl=en&co=GENIE.Platform%3DDesktop)
- Otter: Hebrew listed under "Coming soon" on Nov 10, 2026; the older help article still says English only. — [blog](https://otter.ai/blog/otter-ai-announces-its-biggest-multilingual-leap-yet-27-new-languages-and-multilingual-capabilities); [help, stale](https://help.otter.ai/hc/en-us/articles/360047247414-Supported-languages)
- Fathom: "We currently support transcripts in 38 languages!"; Hebrew not listed. — [Fathom help](https://help.fathom.video/en/articles/296192)
- Read AI: the March 4, 2026 post lists "Greek, Hebrew, Hindi" under the languages Read natively supports ("more than 25 languages"); the support article on languages failed to load. — [Read AI](https://www.read.ai/post/six-new-languages-now-supported)
- Notion AI Meeting Notes: Hebrew is in the speaker-label list but not in the FAQ list for "What language is AI Meeting Notes available for?". — [Notion help](https://www.notion.com/help/ai-meeting-notes)

### Inferences
- Otter is marked "לא נתמכת לפי המקור" because the official source lists Hebrew as not yet available. Fathom is marked "המקור לא מזכיר עברית", because its list omits Hebrew without saying so.
