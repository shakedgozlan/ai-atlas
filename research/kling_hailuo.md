## 4. Strengths and limitations
Third-party 2026 reviews were located but could NOT be opened (fetch budget exhausted). All points below come from official sources.

Strengths
1. Video with audio generated in one pass. "H3 generates video with synchronized stereo audio in the same pass", clips "up to 15 seconds", up to 2K via the API. — https://design.minimax.io/h3 (undated page)
2. Heavy reference control. The Ref2VA variant accepts up to 9 image, 3 video and 3 audio references; FL2VA supports first and/or last frame control. — same source
3. Open weights available. H3 weights are free under the "MiniMax H3 Community License", "royalty-free, for both research and commercial use", hosted on Hugging Face, ModelScope and GitHub. — same source

Limitations
1. Open weights are restricted. Not available in the EU, UK, US or South Korea; companies above US$20 million yearly revenue need separate written authorisation; commercial products must display "MiniMax H3"; native open-weight output is 768p on the short side. — https://design.minimax.io/h3
2. Free downloads are watermarked and credits expire fast. "Video downloads will include a watermark" for free users; new-user bonus credits "will expire three days after being granted"; membership credits "do not roll over to the next month"; no refunds for payments made. — https://hailuoai.video/doc/payment-policy.html (last updated 7/14/2025; OLD SOURCE)
3. Broad, irrevocable content licence and unclear training position. Terms grant a "royalty-free, perpetual, irrevocable, worldwide, non-exclusive" licence over user contributions and generated content; neither the terms nor the privacy policy says whether content is used for model training. — https://hailuoai.video/doc/terms-of-service.html (Last Modified 08/19/2026) ; https://hailuoai.video/doc/privacy-policy.html (Updated December 9, 2025; old source)
