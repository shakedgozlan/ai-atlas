## Key question 1: Review-platform scores and repeated praise/complaint themes per tool

### Takeaway
On B2B review sites (G2/Capterra) the editing, avatar and meeting tools score high (4.4-5.0) and complaints centre on price and accuracy, while consumer-facing review text (Trustpilot, app-store reviews) for credit-based generators and music tools is dominated by credits lost on failed outputs, refusals to refund and hard cancellation. Fathom has by far the largest and highest G2 base in meetings (5.0 from ~7.1k reviews); Fireflies beats Otter on G2 (4.7 vs 4.4).

## Key question 3: First-person long-term experience and creator opinion

### Takeaway
The few genuine first-person accounts found are sceptical about the credit-based video aggregators (Higgsfield "not worth it") and positive on botless meeting notes (Granola kept after months), but first-person sources are thin and several carry affiliate links.

### Cited Findings
- Higgsfield (Taylor Lorenz, User Mag, 30 Jul 2026): saw "no less than five upgrade ads" before starting; creator plan billed annually ("$840!"); bought $129/month Ultra; fan-cam result "did not look like me"; edit uploads failed so she cropped in CapCut; cancelling meant clicking through five promotional offers, with 79% of credits used; upsells for "Kling 3.0 UNLIMITED"; verdict: until these are "truly consumer friendly, they're simply not worth it"; similar clips could "probably" be made free on Grok. READ — [User Mag](https://www.usermag.co/p/my-journey-into-the-slop-factory)
- Granola vs Otter (Zachary Proser, 2 Aug 2026): "I used Granola and Otter.ai side by side for months", kept Granola and continues to use it; notes that Otter's desktop app can now also record without sending a bot, so "no bot" is no longer unique to Granola. Affiliate disclosure: Granola is an affiliate partner. READ — [Zack Proser](https://zackproser.com/blog/granola-vs-otter-which-is-better)
- Wispr Flow users (Product Hunt): "Really hands-down the best AI dictation tool on a Mac" (1 yr ago); a paying Windows user reports setup problems and week-long support delay. READ — [Product Hunt](https://www.producthunt.com/products/wisprflow/reviews?page=2)
- Descript: a reviewer reports lost edits and "We lost many hours of work" (Jan 2026); another praises transcript editing as "superb". READ — [Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/230702/Descript/reviews/)
- Creator comparisons exist for Kling 4.0 vs Seedance 2.5 (Curious Refuge) and Seedance vs Veo vs Kling (Kapwing); already covered in video-report.md. INFERRED (titles) — [Curious Refuge](https://curiousrefuge.com/blog/kling-vs-seedance), [Kapwing](https://www.kapwing.com/resources/seedance-vs-veo-vs-kling-text-to-video-comparison-2026)

### Inferences
- For design students, the Lorenz account is the clearest warning: aggregators bundle many models but push upsells and annual billing; a monthly plan and checking the free Grok/Flow options first is the safer path (INFERRED).

## Hebrew support (categories 7, 8, 13)

### Takeaway
No user report was found confirming good Hebrew in any of the listed tools; the only independent Hebrew evidence is an academic ASR benchmark (2025, STALE) showing commercial and open models differ greatly in Hebrew error rates, and a 2025 Israeli article noting NotebookLM's Hebrew audio still mispronounces.

### Cited Findings
- Hebrew ASR benchmark (Interspeech, Aug 2025, Weizmann Institute / Hadassah / ivrit.ai; STALE): word error rate on conversational Hebrew (eval-d1): ivrit.ai Whisper 6.2, AWS Transcribe Batch 6.9, OpenAI Whisper Large v2 8.0, Large v3 9.6, Google Speech 21.2. Google Speech worst on every set (18.9-38.5). The open ivrit.ai model is the best open-source option. READ — [ISCA archive](https://www.isca-archive.org/interspeech_2025/marmor25_interspeech.pdf). (Answers the earlier round's open question: ivrit.ai exists.)
- NotebookLM Audio Overviews in Hebrew (Jerusalem Post, Barak Ben Hamo, 17 May 2025, STALE): "one may still encounter occasional translation errors or mispronunciations"; no first-person test. READ — [JPost](https://www.jpost.com/consumerism/article-852808)
- OpusClip: G2 lists 20 languages, no Hebrew. READ — [G2](https://www.g2.com/products/opusclip/reviews)
- Wispr Flow: claims 100+ languages and mixed-language dictation (vendor); users praise mixing languages in one sentence; no Hebrew mention in Product Hunt or App Store reviews. READ — [Product Hunt](https://www.producthunt.com/products/wisprflow/reviews?page=2), [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/ae/app/id6497229487)
- Speechify, Descript (Capterra), Udio: no Hebrew mentions in reviews read. READ — [Trustpilot](https://uk.trustpilot.com/review/speechify.com?page=3), [Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/230702/Descript/reviews/)

INFERRED — e.g. [CapCut help: inaccurate auto-captions](https://www.capcut.com/he-il/help/auto-captions-in-capcut), [HeyGen Hebrew](https://www.heygen.com/he-il/translate/english-to-hebrew)
