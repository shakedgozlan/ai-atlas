# User experience and reviews: images (3), graphic design and branding (4), UI and web building (5), 3D (9). November 2026

All pages read on 2026-11-18. READ = seen on the fetched page. INFERRED = from a search-result title or URL only (not opened). STALE = the evidence is older than 6 months (before mid-May 2026). Reddit was not used. Trustpilot pages were fetched from one listing page each (about 15-20 reviews visible), so the themes are a sample. The TrustScore, the review count and the star distribution are profile-wide. Budget: 25 fetches, all used (one failed).

Method caveat for the report writer: Trustpilot scores here run in two directions. Tools that invite reviews (Meshy, Leonardo, Kittl, Looka, Tripo, Freepik/Magnific) score 4.4-4.7 with many short 5-star reviews that often praise support agents by name. Tools that do not invite (Midjourney, Recraft, Webflow) collect mostly angry billing reviews and score 1.5-2.4. Neither number is a quality verdict on its own. The repeated complaint themes are more useful than the score (INFERRED from the "invites customers"/"hasn't recently invited" flags quoted below).

## Q1. Per tool: review-platform scores, counts and dates, plus repeated praise and complaint themes

### Takeaway
Across all four categories, the one complaint users repeat is credit burn: credits are charged for failed, refused or bad generations, and there are no refunds. Users report this for Figma Make, Adobe Firefly, Recraft, Krea, Ideogram and Midjourney. The tools users clearly love are rare. In this set, user text shows real enthusiasm only for Kittl (design), Tripo/Meshy (3D, but on invited reviews) and the ease of use of Canva and Wix. Midjourney, Recraft and Webflow have openly hostile Trustpilot profiles.

### Inferences
- For the atlas tags, "worthwhile" and "recommended" should not rest on Trustpilot scores for Meshy, Leonardo, Kittl, Looka or Tripo alone, because those profiles are invitation-driven. The repeated complaint themes are the more honest signal.
- Credit burn on failed or refused generations is the main designer-facing risk in 2026 across Adobe, Figma, Recraft, Krea, Ideogram and Midjourney. A student tag such as "credits run out fast" is supported for Figma Make, Krea and Recraft by multiple independent users.
- Recraft is still the tool reviewers name for vectors, but its user-facing billing reputation is poor (75% 1-star, all within the last 2 months). The atlas should pair any "recommended for vectors" tag with a billing warning.
- Website builders (Wix, Webflow, and Framer on pricing) draw complaints about lock-in and add-on pricing more than about the AI features. The AI is rarely the reason users praise or leave them.

### Gaps
- No G2 or Capterra scores were collected for Canva, Adobe Express, Figma, Framer, Webflow, Microsoft Designer, Midjourney, Recraft, Spline or Hitem3D (fetch budget). G2 often blocks the fetcher.
- There is no user-review platform for Gemini/Nano Banana or FLUX as such. User opinion about them lives in general chatbot app reviews and in social posts that were not reachable.
- Luma Genie, Hyper3D Rodin, Relume and Microsoft Designer: no real first-person user evidence found.
- Hebrew support: no user in any fetched review mentioned Hebrew, either text in images or RTL in Canva/Wix.

## Q4. Per category: which tool users say they switched TO or AWAY from in 2026, and why

### Takeaway
The switching evidence is thin and mostly single-user. The clearest pattern is in UI/web: away from Figma Make credits toward BYOK coding agents and Google AI Studio, and away from Webflow, Wix and Framer over add-on pricing and lock-in. ## Q5. Per category: which free plan users call usable, which paid plan users call good value, and student experiences

### Takeaway
Users explicitly praise only Tripo's free credits. Elsewhere, the free and paid experience is dominated by complaints about credits running out. No first-person student evidence was found, except dated Uizard reviews calling it costly for students.

### Cited Findings
- **Tripo (3D).** "Users get more than enough free credits to get a hang of the tool" (May 15 2026), and "The pricing is good" (May 17 2026). READ. — [Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/tripo3d.ai?page=6)
- **Hitem3D (3D).** Free trial credits, paid plans from $9.90/month, and 3 free retries (Feb 2026, STALE). READ. — [All3DP](https://all3dp.com/4/new-ai-image-to-model-generator-promises-perfect-stl-files-we-put-it-to-the-test/)
- **Magnific (images).** A subscriber values the "unlimited generation" option as the main reason to pay (Sep 4 2026). READ. — [Trustpilot](https://ca.trustpilot.com/review/freepik.com?page=8)
- **Figma Make (UI).** About $16/month includes 3,000 credits, which users say lasts "about 3 days" of active work. Top-ups cost $60 for 2,500 credits, about 5x more per credit (May 2026). READ. — [Figma Forum](https://forum.figma.com/share-your-feedback-26/figma-make-ai-credit-limits-not-feasible-51713/index19.html)
- **HN on Figma Make.** "at least useful for the free tokens" (about Oct 2026). READ. — [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49923960)
- **Ideogram (images).** The free plan changed weekly (Apr 2025, STALE). READ. — [Trustpilot](https://uk.trustpilot.com/review/ideogram.ai?page=8)
- **Krea (images).** "to much expensive" (Jun 14 2026), and confusing tiers at sign-up (Oct 1 2026). READ. — [Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/www.krea.ai)
- **Canva (design).** Paywalled premium elements and background removal are recurring complaints (Oct 2026). READ. — [Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/canva.com?page=1)
- **Framer (web).** Base site plan $15/month, plus $40 per language and $20 per editor; the agency user says the price is "hard to justify". READ (undated). — [feedme.design](https://feedme.design/lets-talk-about-framers-pricing)
- **Students.** Uizard was "a bit expensive" and costly for students (2021-2022, STALE). This is the only student-specific user text found. READ. — [Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/170822/Uizard/reviews)

### Inferences
- In 3D, Tripo is the only tool with a user-backed "worthwhile free plan" signal, and it comes from invited reviews. In images, Magnific's unlimited tier is the only paid plan users praise for value.
- None of the fetched sources supports a "good value" tag for Recraft, Krea, Figma Make credits or Webflow. For each, user text says the opposite.

### Gaps
- Student-specific experiences with Figma Education, Canva for Education, Adobe student pricing and Framer/Webflow education plans were not found in user text. This needs a targeted pass, for example on the Figma forum "education" tag and the Adobe Community.
- There is no user evidence on whether the free tiers of Gemini/Nano Banana, ChatGPT images or Microsoft Designer are usable.
- Licensing and commercial use as users experience it: only one unverified Recraft claim (ownership after the subscription lapses). There are no user reports on Midjourney, Firefly or Ideogram commercial-use disputes.
