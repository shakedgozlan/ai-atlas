## Part C — Which extra criteria would be useful and measurable on a tool card?

### Takeaway
Reviewers and leaderboards compare video tools on arena score, price per second or minute, clip length, resolution, native audio, image-to-video support, open weights and licence, speed and best-for use case; the evidence gathered here adds usage-based and trust-based criteria (popularity evidence, app-store rating, commercial rights, training on user data). Twelve criteria below are measurable from public pages.

### Cited Findings
- A third-party ranking (published June 27, 2026, updated November 6, 2026) compares models on: arena score (Artificial Analysis Elo), maker, max clip length, native audio, image-to-video support, resolution, open weights and licence, best-for use case, price (per second or per plan), VRAM / where to run (open models), and speed. READ. — [Techsy](https://techsy.io/en/blog/best-ai-video-models)
- Artificial Analysis publishes, per model: Elo from blind human votes, release month, and price to generate one minute of video on the creator's API (examples: Wan 3.0 US$12.00, MiniMax H3 768p US$4.80, Seedance 2.5 US$34.12, Gemini Omni Flash 1.2 US$6.26, Pika H3 Flex US$2.10 per minute). It keeps separate boards for video with audio and silent video. READ. — [Artificial Analysis](https://artificialanalysis.ai/video/leaderboard/text-to-video)
- App-store rating can diverge sharply from popularity: Kling was #1 in its App Store category while its seven-day average rating was 1.4 stars with 90% one-star reviews (May 2026, MWM estimates; STALE). READ. — [MWM](https://mwm.ai/articles/kling-ai-tops-app-store-charts-in-may-2026-following-sora-shutdown)
- Whether a tool owns its model or resells others' matters to analysts: Higgsfield "builds on models it does not own"; Magnific is described as model-agnostic. READ. — [The Next Web, Higgsfield](https://thenextweb.com/news/higgsfield-series-b-400m-5-4bn-valuation-700m-revenue); [The Next Web, Magnific](https://thenextweb.com/news/freepik-rebrands-as-magnific)
- Official pages already yield per-tool facts on watermark removal by plan, credits per second, refund window, output ownership, commercial-use wording, training on user content and operating entity (shown for Dreamina in the earlier file). — [Dreamina pricing](https://dreamina.capcut.com/seedance/seedance-2-0-pricing); [Dreamina terms](https://dreamina.capcut.com/clause/dreamina-terms-of-service?lang=en); [Dreamina privacy policy](https://dreamina.capcut.com/clause/dreamina-privacy-policy)
- Reviewers also judge qualitative behaviour: artifacts, physics errors, motion stiffness, multi-shot ability, lip sync, character consistency and camera controls (earlier file and the ranking above). — [Curious Refuge](https://curiousrefuge.com/blog/best-ai-video-generators-2026); [Techsy](https://techsy.io/en/blog/best-ai-video-models)

### Inferences
Suggested 12 criteria (each with where the evidence would come from):
1. **Real cost per second of finished video** on the cheapest paid plan (credits per second × plan price; vendor pricing page) — more honest than the monthly price.
2. **Free plan reality**: daily/monthly free credits, watermark on free output, and whether the top model is included (vendor pricing page).
3. **Max clip length and max resolution** per generation (vendor docs; reviewer tables).
4. **Native audio**: none / sound effects / speech with lip sync (vendor docs; Artificial Analysis has a separate with-audio board).
5. **Quality rank**: Artificial Analysis arena Elo and rank, with the read date (third-party, blind votes).
6. **Popularity evidence level**: e.g. "strong / partial / none", with the single best dated figure and whether it is vendor or third-party (this file).
7. **Own model or hub**: does the tool run its own model, or let you pick other companies' models (vendor site; press).
8. **Commercial use and ownership**: allowed on which plan, and who owns outputs (terms of service).
9. **Training on your uploads**: yes / no / opt-out (privacy policy).
10. **Control features**: start/end frame, reference images for character consistency, camera controls, multi-shot (vendor docs; reviews).
11. **Model freshness**: name and release month of the current flagship model (leaderboard release column; vendor news) — the field moves monthly.
12. **Company and jurisdiction**: maker, country, and the legal entity for users in Israel (terms of service) — plus, optionally, **app-store rating** and **failed-generation / refund policy** where published.
- Criteria that look attractive but were not shown to be measurable from public evidence in this session: content-filter strictness (no public metric found), generation speed (only mentioned in passing by reviewers; no standard public benchmark read), review-platform scores (no G2/Trustpilot pages read).

### Gaps
- Only one dedicated comparison article could be read for criteria (MarkTechPost's November 13, 2026 piece failed to load); the criteria list rests on that article, the leaderboard's columns and the earlier file's review.
- No source was found that measures content-filter strictness, generation speed or failed-generation refunds in a comparable way across tools.
- Whether Artificial Analysis publishes generation-speed data for video models was not checked.
