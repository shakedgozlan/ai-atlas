# Categories 8 (Voice, music and sound) and 9 (3D): which tools independent reviewers actually recommend, Nov 2026

Research date: 2026-11-18. Budget used: 22 of 22 page fetches (3 failed at the site: boingboing.net, cybernews.com, thenextweb.com). Not used: reddit, YouTube, PCMag, CNET, diyai.io.
Labels: **READ** = I saw it on the fetched page. **INFERRED** = taken only from a search-result title or URL. **Assumption** = not verified.
Staleness flag: anything dated before 2026-05-18 is marked **(STALE >6 mo)**.

Source weighting used below:
- High: Artificial Analysis arenas (blind votes), Zapier (3+ weeks hands-on, Sept 2026), All3DP (hands-on with real prints, June 2026), a16z Top 100 (traffic/spend data).
- Medium: SoftPicker (tested, but Mar 2026 = STALE), ToolChase (has a scoring framework, but affiliate links and a paid "featured partner"), TechRadar (tested, Feb 2026 = STALE), Curious Refuge (tested SFX, Jan 2026 = STALE).
- Low: eesel.ai (a Suno-only review on a site that promotes its own products), IndieHackers "I tested 9" (anonymous, reads as promotion for Hyper3D/Rodin), Sloyd blog arena write-up (Sloyd ranks itself #3), eWeek 3D (no date, tool set looks 2023-24 era), Cinevva timeline (a compilation of vendor posts, useful only for release dates).
