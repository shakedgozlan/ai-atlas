## Tool 1: Luma AI — Luma / Luma Agents (Ray3.2); formerly Dream Machine

### Takeaway
The product is now branded "Luma" with the app called "Luma Agents"; Dream Machine is the legacy product, closed to new signups. Current video model is Ray3.2 (June 9, 2026). Paid plans are Plus $30, Pro $90, Ultra $300 per month; the official terms give Luma training rights over inputs and outputs, including for paid subscribers.

**4. Strengths and limitations**

Strengths
- Frame-level direction with many keyframes. Ray3.2 supports up to 16 keyframes per clip for controlling pacing, motion and camera paths (official) — [Ray3.2 announcement](https://lumalabs.ai/news/introducing-ray-3-2)
- HDR output for post-production. Ray3.2 generates native HDR with 16-bit EXR export, at 1080p, clips up to 20 seconds (official) — [Ray3.2 announcement](https://lumalabs.ai/news/introducing-ray-3-2). A third-party review (Tom Langridge, fast.io, updated Jun 26, 2026) also names native 16-bit HDR/EXR and keyframe control as strengths, and rates image-to-video highly (reviewer opinion, written about Ray3) — [fast.io review](https://fast.io/resources/luma-ai-review-2026/)
- Change the shot after generation. "Enhanced Reframe" changes aspect ratio, extends the frame or replaces a background while keeping the original lighting; performance tracking captures posture, gestures and facial expression for up to eight faces (official) — [Ray3.2 announcement](https://lumalabs.ai/news/introducing-ray-3-2)

Limitations
- Short clips. Official maximum is 20 seconds per clip for Ray3.2 — [Ray3.2 announcement](https://lumalabs.ai/news/introducing-ray-3-2); third-party review says longer content needs external stitching (it cites an 18-second limit for Ray3) — [fast.io review](https://fast.io/resources/luma-ai-review-2026/)
- Fine detail is unreliable (third-party). Reviewer reports inconsistent hands and fingers, unreliable text inside video, and trouble with specific human actions and precise spatial reasoning — [fast.io review](https://fast.io/resources/luma-ai-review-2026/)
- Credits do not carry over and high settings are costly. Unused credits expire at the end of the billing cycle (official) — [lumalabs.ai/app](https://lumalabs.ai/app); a 10-second 1080p Ray3.2 clip costs 1,200 credits and HDR is 2x, HDR+EXR 3x the SDR rate (official API price table; the Plus plan has 10,000 credits) — [lumalabs.ai/api](https://lumalabs.ai/api), [lumalabs.ai/pricing](https://lumalabs.ai/pricing)
- Conflict to record: the third-party review says there is no built-in audio (written about Dream Machine/Ray3) — [fast.io review](https://fast.io/resources/luma-ai-review-2026/); the official Luma Agents page lists voiceover, sound effects, music and lip sync in the workspace — [lumalabs.ai/app](https://lumalabs.ai/app); the Ray3.2 announcement itself does not mention audio — [Ray3.2 announcement](https://lumalabs.ai/news/introducing-ray-3-2)

## Tool 2: Pika (pika.art)

### Takeaway
Pika is now a multi-model creative platform (video, image, audio) with its own Pika 2.5 and Pika H3 Flex models alongside third-party models. Official plans (updated October 28, 2026): Free $0 with zero monthly credits, Starter $10, Creator $35, Fancy from $95 per month; all plans are watermark-free but a commercial licence starts at Starter. Terms allow training on anonymised or aggregated content with no general opt-out.

- Own video models named on the homepage: Pika 2.5 and Pika H3 Flex (described as a faster-than-realtime model built for iteration), plus Pikaframes. Third-party video models offered: Seedance 2.5 (and 4K), Kling 4.0, Wan 3.0, MiniMax H3, Gemini Omni 1.1 Flash — [pika.art](https://pika.art/)

**4. Strengths and limitations**

Strengths
- Many models in one place. One account gives access to Pika's own models and third-party video, image and audio models (official); the pricing page lists "All models" on every plan including Free — [pika.art](https://pika.art/), [pika.art/pricing](https://pika.art/pricing)
- No watermark on any plan. The official pricing page lists "No watermark" for Free, Starter, Creator, Fancy and Enterprise — [pika.art/pricing](https://pika.art/pricing)
- Easy and fast for short creative clips (third-party). A review by Steven Jones, DIY AI, August 17, 2026, calls Pika "still one of the easiest AI video generators to use", praises render speed and the effects toolkit (Pikaffects, Pikaswaps, Pikadditions, Pikatwists, Pikaformance) and image-to-video. Note: this review predates the current pricing — [DIY AI review](https://diyai.io/ai-tools/video-generation/reviews/pika-ai-review/)

Limitations
- Free plan cannot generate without paying. Free has 0 monthly credits ("credit packs only") and no commercial licence (official) — [pika.art/pricing](https://pika.art/pricing)
- Weak consistency across shots (third-party). Faces, outfits and object shapes can drift between shots; motion can deform fine details; logos and labels need frame-by-frame checks — [DIY AI review](https://diyai.io/ai-tools/video-generation/reviews/pika-ai-review/)
- Not a full editing workspace, and high resolution burns credits (third-party). The reviewer says the transformation tools are not a mature editing or project-management workspace, and that 1080p and effects cost several times more credits (e.g. 5s clip: 12 credits at 480p, 40 at 1080p — figures from August 2026, not rechecked against the current official credit table) — [DIY AI review](https://diyai.io/ai-tools/video-generation/reviews/pika-ai-review/)
- Top-up credits are limited in scope (official): they work only on Pika Create (pika.art) and MCP, not the API, the iOS app or old.pika.art — [pika.art/pricing](https://pika.art/pricing)
