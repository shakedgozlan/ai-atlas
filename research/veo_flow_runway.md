## 4. Strengths and limitations (all from official pages; no third-party review loaded)
Strengths (documented capabilities, not proof of superiority):
1. Several input modes in one tool. With Omni Flash 1.2, Flow offers Text to Video, Frames to Video (web only), Ingredients/References to Video and Video to Video editing. https://support.google.com/flow/answer/16352836?hl=en
2. A daily free allowance without a subscription. Users with no Google AI subscription get "50 Google Flow credits per day". https://support.google.com/flow/answer/16526234?hl=en
3. Longer clips, extension and upscaling on paid plans. Paid plans get 3 to 15 second clips, Omni Extend (3 to 10 second extensions) and upscaling of Omni 360p videos to 1080p, with 4K for Ultra. https://support.google.com/flow/answer/16352836?hl=en
- Additional documented capability: Omni Flash models include "Create custom voices" (choose a preset voice, then describe how to style it). https://support.google.com/flow/answer/16352836?hl=en

Limitations (documented limits):
1. Models are being retired on short notice. "On December 5, 2026, Veo 3.1 Fast, Veo 3.1 Quality, and Omni Flash 1.1 will be retired." and "On November 5, 2026, Veo Extend was retired." https://support.google.com/flow/answer/16352836?hl=en
2. Free use can be blocked at busy times and is short. Without a subscription, video generation may be unavailable during peak hours (roughly 2 PM to 5 PM UTC), clips are 3 to 10 seconds, and unused daily credits do not roll over. https://support.google.com/flow/answer/16526234?hl=en and https://support.google.com/flow/answer/16352836?hl=en
3. Veo models are short and restricted. Veo 3.1 Lite is 720p at 4, 6 or 8 seconds with no Video to Video or Extend; Veo 3.1 Quality has no Ingredients/References, Video to Video or Extend. https://support.google.com/flow/answer/16352836?hl=en
- Further documented limits: some Omni features and voice customisations are only available in certain regions; https://support.google.com/flow/answer/16352836?hl=en . Browsers other than Chromium-based ones (Chrome, Edge) are "not yet fully optimized"; https://support.google.com/flow/answer/16353333 . The service is for users aged 18+; https://support.google.com/flow/answer/16353544?hl=en

## 4. Strengths and limitations (all from official pages; no third-party review loaded)
Strengths (documented capabilities, not proof of superiority):

3. Unused credits are lost on the cheaper plans. Standard and Pro monthly credits do not roll over; only Max (and Team, per the help page) roll over up to one month. https://runway.com/pricing and https://help.runwayml.com/hc/en-us/articles/21664961171475-Which-plan-is-right-for-me
- Further documented limits: Free users get only a limited, changing selection of models and 5 GB storage; https://help.runwayml.com/hc/en-us/articles/50404627334547-Free-plan-details . The Usage Policy bans, among others, using someone's image without permission and imitating a living artist's style; https://help.runwayml.com/hc/en-us/articles/17944787368595 (last updated August 4, 2025, OLD SOURCE).
